01The Gap

Your business changed.Your digital presence didn't.

It happens quietly. The company grows on referrals and reputation, year after year — and the website, the Google profile, the logo, the email address all stay where they were when things started.

Then one day a serious customer searches your name, finds a site from another era, hours that are wrong, a Facebook page nobody can log into — and calls a competitor who simply looks more established. Not better. Just more visible.

The work is excellent. The presence doesn't say so. That gap costs real opportunities.

08About

Built around one observation.

AMRI Group is a founder-led Atlanta studio built around a simple observation: some of the best businesses we meet look nothing like themselves online.

The work is strong. The reputation is real. But the website is a decade behind, the accounts are scattered across old providers, the brand has drifted, and nobody is quite sure who owns what. Fixing the website alone doesn't fix that — the system around it stays broken.

So that's what we build: brand, web and digital infrastructure brought into one professional system that finally reflects the business behind it — designed carefully, documented completely, and handed back to the people it belongs to.

We help businesses look as established online as they are in real life.
